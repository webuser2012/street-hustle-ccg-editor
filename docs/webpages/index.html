Getting Started

HOW TO
PLAY

The CCG is easy to learn in five steps. The digital version is instant — just open and play.

01 🏙️

Play Boroughs

Each turn, play a Borough card to expand your operation and generate Heat — the resource you spend to play all other cards.

02 🧑

Deploy Dealers

Spend Heat to put Dealers into your Active zone. They're your fighters — but they can't do anything without product.

03 💊

Load Stash

Attach drug cards to your Active Dealer to boost their ATK. No Stash = no damage. That's the Supply Chain Rule.

04 ⚔️

Attack

Your loaded Dealer hits the opponent's dealer. Damage = ATK minus their DEF. Drop their HP to zero for a KO.

05 🏆

Win

KO 5 dealers to collect all Prize Cards for an instant win — or grind their HP to zero. First to 0 HP loses.

Core Systems

WHAT MAKES
IT TICK

Six interlocking mechanics built from the ground up to capture the authentic risk and reward of street economics.

⛓️

Supply Chain Rule

Dealers deal 0 damage without Stash attached. You must load product before every attack. One exception: Ghost operates on reputation alone.

🚔

Bust Mechanic

Carry 4+ Stash on a Dealer and a Police Bust hits for +3 bonus damage — then strips everything. Safe maximum is 3 cards.

🏆

Prize System

KO an opponent's Dealer and draw a Prize Card. Collect all 5 and win instantly. But Prize cards can't be played the turn you earn them.

🔥

Heat Economy

Your Boroughs generate Heat each turn. Spend it on Dealers, Stash, and Actions. Expand your borough footprint to spend more every turn.

📈

Market Shift

A free Action that makes all Stash cost 0 Heat for both players this turn — a sudden price spike that can flip the board state in a single turn.

👊

Bare Knuckle

No Active Dealer? You still deal 1 direct HP damage. There's no such thing as a completely wasted turn on the street.

The Deck

53 CARDS
4 TYPES

Every card in the 40-card starter deck serves the economy. From cheap Common crew to Legendary game-enders.

Dealers

10 unique · Common to Legendary

Your fighters. From the 1-cost Street Punk to the 5-cost Kingpin. Each needs Stash to operate — except Ghost, who works on rep alone.

Stash

8 types · Weed to Golden Brick

Drug cards that attach to your Dealer and boost ATK. Free Ludes heal. Mystery Stash rolls a die. The Golden Brick (+5 ATK) hits when it lands.

Actions

16 types · Free to 4 Heat

Instant effects: draw, heal, deal direct damage, freeze a dealer, bust their stash, or drop a Federal Raid to wipe the board.

Boroughs

6 NYC locations · Free to play

Bronx, Brooklyn, Queens, Staten Island, Harlem, Manhattan. Free to play each turn. Each generates Heat and provides a unique territory bonus.
